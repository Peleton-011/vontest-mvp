# Social Game Concepts Documentation

## Overview
This document outlines deliberative and social game concepts designed to foster meaningful interaction, discussion, and connection between users. Each game balances lighthearted fun with opportunities for deeper engagement.

---

## Core Design Principles

### Key Ingredients for Success
- **Regular Cadence**: Daily or weekly prompts to build habit and anticipation
- **Discussion-First**: Voting/answers unlock conversations, not just results
- **Group Dynamics**: Reveals how friends think and relate to each other
- **Mixed Depth**: Balance between silly fun and meaningful topics
- **Delayed Gratification**: Staggered reveals to build suspense
- **Low Barrier**: Quick to participate, deep to engage

## Game Concepts

### 1. Would You Rather: Ranked

**Tagline**: "It's not just what you choose—it's how strongly you feel about it"

#### Core Mechanic
- Daily "would you rather" question presented to the group
- Users choose option A or B AND rate intensity (1-10 scale)
- After everyone votes, see full distribution curve and outliers
- Dedicated chat thread for discussion

#### Example Questions
- "Would you rather have the ability to fly OR be invisible?"
- "Would you rather always be 10 minutes late OR always be 20 minutes early?"
- "Would you rather give up cheese forever OR give up bread forever?"

#### Key Features
- **Intensity Heatmap**: Visual showing who feels strongest about their choice
- **Outlier Spotlight**: Highlight the 10/10 passionate votes
- **Flip Detection**: Track who changes their mind in discussion
- **Historical Stats**: "You've been Team A 73% of the time"

#### Discussion Prompts
- Auto-generate: "Why did 3 people rate this a 10?"
- Pair up: "Sarah and Mike both chose differently but rated 9—debate it out!"

### 2. Hot Takes

**Tagline**: "Defend your (mildly controversial) opinions"

#### Core Mechanic
- Daily polarizing but playful statement posted
- Vote: Strongly Agree / Agree / Disagree / Strongly Disagree
- Those on extreme opposite ends get matched for mini-debates
- Community votes on "most convincing argument"

#### Example Hot Takes
- "Pineapple belongs on pizza"
- "Cats are better than dogs"
- "The book is always better than the movie"
- "Cold pizza is better than hot pizza"
- "Cereal is a soup"

#### Key Features
- **Debate Matchmaking**: Auto-pair strongest opposing views
- **Argument Voting**: Non-debaters vote on winner
- **Conviction Score**: Leaderboard for most persuasive debaters
- **Hot Take Submissions**: Users can submit their own for group voting
- **Devil's Advocate Mode**: Randomly assigned to argue opposite view

#### Debate Structure
1. Opening statements (60 seconds to write)
2. Rebuttals (30 seconds each)
3. Closing argument (30 seconds)
4. Group votes on winner

---

### 3. Guess Who Said It

**Tagline**: "How well do you really know your friends?"

#### Core Mechanic
- Weekly submission window: everyone anonymously submits confession/opinion/story
- Voting phase: guess who said what
- Reveal phase: see correct answers + chat about surprises
- Points for accurate guesses

#### Submission Categories
- **Confessions**: "I've never actually seen [popular movie]"
- **Unpopular Opinions**: "I think [controversial take]"
- **Secret Talents**: "I can [unusual skill]"
- **Life Stories**: "One time I [interesting anecdote]"
- **Future Dreams**: "If money wasn't an issue, I'd [ambition]"

#### Key Features
- **Confidence Levels**: Rate how sure you are (1-5 stars)
- **Bonus points for correct high-confidence guesses**
- **Group Stats**: "You know Sarah best—87% accuracy"
- **Anonymous Until Reveal**: Encourages honesty
- **Surprise Meter**: Highlights most unexpected reveals

#### Scoring
- Correct guess: 10 points
- Confidence bonus: 2 points per star if correct
- Penalty: -1 point per star if wrong (risk/reward)
- "Fooled Everyone" bonus: 20 points if nobody guesses your submission

### 4. Predict Your Friends

**Tagline**: "You think you know them? Prove it."

#### Core Mechanic
- Daily prediction question about group members
- Everyone predicts, then one random person gives "true answer"
- Points for accuracy
- Discussion about why people predicted what they did

#### Example Predictions
- "Who will text back first in the next hour?"
- "Who's most likely to cancel plans this weekend?"
- "Who will be the first to watch the new episode?"
- "Who's currently thinking about food?"
- "Who will stay up latest tonight?"

#### Key Features
- **Real-Time Validation**: Some questions auto-verify (text-back timing)
- **Oracle Rotation**: Different person is "truth-teller" each day
- **Prediction Streaks**: Track consecutive correct predictions
- **Self-Awareness Score**: Compare self-predictions vs. group predictions
- **Category Tracking**: "Best at predicting who's hungry: Mike"

#### Question Types
- Behavioral predictions (observable)
- Opinion predictions (requires oracle)
- Ranking predictions (order multiple people)
- Time-based predictions (who will X first)

### 5. Dinner Party Dilemmas

**Tagline**: "Curate the most interesting conversation"

#### Core Mechanic
- Weekly challenge: invite 3 out of 8 figures to your dinner party
- Mix of historical figures, fictional characters, celebrities
- Submit picks with required reasoning
- Group votes on whose party would be most interesting
- Debate thread for each submission

#### Example Guest Lists
Historical: "Einstein, Cleopatra, Shakespeare, MLK Jr., Marie Curie, Napoleon, Leonardo da Vinci, Joan of Arc"
Fictional: "Sherlock Holmes, Tony Stark, Hermione, Walter White, Tyrion Lannister, Rick Sanchez, Princess Leia, Atticus Finch"
Celebrity: "Current famous people, musicians, athletes, etc."

#### Key Features
- **Theme Weeks**: Different categories each week
- **Reasoning Requirement**: Must explain why these 3
- **Conversation Simulation**: AI generates what dinner convo might sound like
- **Guest Synergy**: Bonus points if others agree your 3 would vibe well
- **Wildcard Slot**: One rotating mystery guest everyone must include

#### Judging Criteria
- Most interesting conversations (voted by group)
- Best reasoning
- Most creative combination
- "I'd actually want to attend this"

### 6. Two Truths Roulette

**Tagline**: "Catch up on life while playing detective"

#### Core Mechanic
- Weekly: everyone submits two truths and one lie about their week
- Group votes on which statement is the lie
- After all votes, reveals correct answer
- Points for fooling friends + guessing correctly

#### Example Submissions
- "I started learning guitar / I ran 5 miles / I ate sushi for the first time"
- "I finished a book / I had a job interview / I adopted a cat"
- "I broke my phone / I cooked Thai food / I met up with an old friend"

#### Key Features
- **Master Deceiver**: Leaderboard for best liars
- **Human Lie Detector**: Best at spotting lies
- **Story Expansion**: After reveal, share more details in chat
- **Challenge Mode**: Others can "challenge" if they think you're lying about a truth
- **Photo Proof**: Optional evidence for truths

#### Scoring
- 5 points per person fooled by your lie
- 10 points for correctly identifying the lie
- -5 points for challenging a truth (wrong challenge)
- +15 points for successfully challenging a false truth (caught a liar)

### 7. The Compliment Economy

**Tagline**: "Make appreciation intentional"

#### Core Mechanic
- Each user gets 5 "compliment coins" per week
- Award coins to friends for specific things
- Must include explanation (minimum 20 characters)
- Public leaderboard, but real value is reading the compliments
- Coins expire weekly (use it or lose it)

#### Compliment Categories
- **Everyday Hero**: For small acts of kindness
- **You Made Me Laugh**: Funniest moment
- **Wise Words**: Best advice given
- **Showed Up**: Being there when needed
- **Level Up**: Personal growth observed
- **Wild Card**: Open-ended appreciation

#### Key Features
- **Compliment Feed**: Public stream of all compliments
- **Weekly Digest**: Summary of who got what
- **Save Feature**: Bookmark favorite compliments
- **Streak Bonus**: Extra coin for 3+ weeks of active participation
- **No Hoarding**: Unspent coins vanish (encourages generosity)

#### Social Elements
- React to others' compliments with emojis
- "Second this" button to amplify compliments
- Monthly: "Most appreciated member"
- Notification: "You just made someone's day!"

### 8. Bracket Battle Royale

**Tagline**: "Settle debates tournament-style"

#### Core Mechanic
- Create single-elimination tournaments for any topic
- 8 or 16 competitors per bracket
- Group debates each matchup before voting
- Winners advance until final champion

#### Example Tournaments
- Best pizza topping
- Most overrated movie
- Worst chore
- Best day of the week
- Superior breakfast food
- Ultimate comfort food
- Best season
- Most useful superpower

#### Key Features
- **Seeding**: Initial ranking by group
- **Upset Alerts**: When underdog wins
- **Final Four Intensity**: Dedicated debate threads for semifinals
- **Championship Ceremony**: Winner gets crowned with custom flair
- **Bracket History**: Archive of past winners
- **Rematch Requests**: Vote to run tournaments again

#### Tournament Phases
1. **Nomination** (Day 1): Submit and vote on entries
2. **Seeding** (Day 2): Rank top 8/16
3. **Round 1** (Days 3-4): First matchups
4. **Quarterfinals** (Days 5-6)
5. **Semifinals** (Days 7-8): Intense debates
6. **Finals** (Days 9-10): Championship round

#### Debate Format per Matchup
- 24-hour voting window
- Open discussion thread
- Must comment to vote (no silent voting in finals)
- Upset bonus: Extra points if you backed the underdog

## Implementation Considerations

### Technical Requirements

#### Notification System
- Daily prompts at consistent time
- "Everyone has voted" alerts
- "Results are in" notifications
- "@mention" alerts in discussions
- Weekly recap notifications

#### Data Structure
```
Group
├── Members
├── Active Game
├── Game History
└── Settings (notification preferences, game rotation)

Game Instance
├── Type
├── Status (submission/voting/discussion/archived)
├── Prompt/Question
├── Submissions (user responses)
├── Votes
├── Discussion Thread
└── Timestamps
```

#### Moderation Features
- Flag inappropriate content
- Edit own submissions (before voting closes)
- Delete own messages
- Admin: skip games, reset timers

### User Experience

#### Onboarding Flow
1. Join group via invite link
2. Tutorial of current active game
3. Submit first response
4. Get notified when voting opens

#### Engagement Hooks
- Streak tracking (days participated)
- Personal stats dashboard
- Group compatibility scores
- Achievement badges
- Seasonal leaderboards

#### Accessibility
- Time zone handling (prompt goes out at 9 AM local)
- Offline mode (draft submissions)
- Read receipts (who's seen the prompt)
- "Skip this one" option (no penalty)

### Monetization Ideas (Optional)
- Premium: Custom games/questions
- Premium: Create multiple groups
- Premium: Extended history access
- Premium: Advanced stats/analytics
- Free tier: 1 game rotation per group
- Premium: Multiple concurrent games

## Game Rotation Strategy

### Suggested Schedule
**Weekly Rotation:**
- Monday: Hot Takes
- Tuesday: Predict Your Friends
- Wednesday: Would You Rather: Ranked
- Thursday: Two Truths Roulette (submission day)
- Friday: Guess Who Said It (reveal day)
- Saturday: The Compliment Economy
- Sunday: Dinner Party Dilemmas OR Bracket Battle (alternating weeks)

**Allow Customization:**
- Groups choose which games to include
- Set custom rotation order
- Weekend vs. weekday games
- Pause games during busy weeks

## Growth & Iteration

### Metrics to Track
- Daily active users
- Submission rate (% who answer prompt)
- Discussion engagement (comments per game)
- Retention (return rate next day/week)
- Game preference (which games get most engagement)

### Future Expansion Ideas
- Cross-group tournaments
- AI-generated personalized questions based on group history
- Video response options
- Voice message debates
- Integration with calendars (real predictions)
- Photo-based games
- Collaborative story-building games
- Memory games ("Remember when...")

### Community Features
- Public game library (share great questions)
- Template brackets
- User-submitted game ideas
- Seasonal events (holiday-themed games)

---

## Success Metrics

A game is successful if it:
1. ✅ Gets >80% participation rate
2. ✅ Generates >5 discussion messages per instance
3. ✅ Users request it appears more often
4. ✅ Creates memorable moments (referenced later)
5. ✅ Strengthens group bonds (measured by survey)
